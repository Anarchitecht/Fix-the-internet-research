# Retrieval log

Every paper attempted, the escalation steps tried, and what came back. A paper that failed every step
is listed with its exact DOI so a person can fetch it directly.

The escalation ladder, in order: DBLP or Crossref for the canonical citation; arXiv; IACR ePrint for
cryptographic work; the author's personal or institutional page; the conference proceedings site;
Semantic Scholar; OpenAlex; CORE; CiteSeerX for pre-2010 systems work; the institutional repository
by name, including the author's PhD thesis; the technical-report version carrying the omitted proofs;
then a person.
