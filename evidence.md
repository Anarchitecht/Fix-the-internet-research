# Evidence

One entry per paper, in the schema of `BRIEF.md` §6. Append-only: an entry is never deleted, only
superseded by a later entry that says so.

An entry exists only for a paper whose full text was retrieved. A paper retrieved as an abstract
appears in `retrieval-log.md` under the outcome that describes what was actually received, and no
number from it may be used anywhere.
